Consent

Information We Collect

Important: This application works OFFLINE on your device. We do NOT collect, store, or transmit personal user data to external servers when used in offline/guest mode.

  • No personal information is required to use the encyclopedia, timers, scoreboard, flashcards, or quizzes.
  • Your training logs, belt progression, favorites, and quiz scores are stored locally on your device.
  • The core features function on your device without requiring an internet connection.
  • If you contact us via email, we may receive your email and message content solely for support purposes.

How We Use Your Information

Since all training progress and preferences are stored locally on your device, we do not sell or share your personal information.

  • The encyclopedia, timers, scoreboard, and quizzes function offline.
  • All training features work locally on your device.
  • Your usage remains private and under your control.

Data Storage & Deletion

Local Device Storage: Preferences, belt rank, flashcards, and training logs are saved locally on your device using standard application preferences. You can permanently reset and delete all stored data at any time from Settings → Delete Account & Reset Data or by uninstalling the application.

Content & Martial Arts Safety

Explore the 67 official Kodokan Judo techniques (Gokyo no Waza and Katame-waza), classical Katas, Japanese Judo terminology, IJF referee rules, and belt grading quizzes. All martial arts content is provided for educational and training reference purposes only. Always practice Judo on proper shock-absorbing Tatami mats under the supervision of a qualified instructor. We respect copyrights and do not include any protected content without permission.
