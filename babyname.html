Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how the Baby Name Arabia – أسماء المواليد app (an interactive app for discovering, filtering, and matching Arabic baby names with your partner) handles your information.

Consent

By using the Baby Name Arabia app, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect

Important: We do NOT collect personal identity information such as your real name, phone number, or physical address.

  • No personal contact information is required to use the main features of the app.
  • Anonymous Identifier (Firebase Anonymous Auth): The app assigns a random, anonymous identifier solely to generate a sharing code so you and your partner can compare your favorite baby names.
  • Device Advertising ID (Google AdMob): Used by Google AdMob to display banner and interstitial advertisements and measure ad performance.
  • Local Preferences: Your liked names, filters, language, and theme settings are stored locally on your device using SharedPreferences.
  • If you contact us via email, we may receive your email address and message content solely for customer support purposes.

How We Use Your Information

  • To save your favorite and disliked baby names locally on your device.
  • To allow optional synchronization of liked name IDs with your coparent/partner using an anonymous sharing code.
  • To display advertisements via Google AdMob.
  • Your usage remains private and pseudonymous.

Data Storage & Deletion

Your preferences are stored locally on your device. If you use the Partner Sharing feature, only your anonymous user ID and the list of numeric IDs of your liked names are stored in Google Cloud Firestore.

Instant Data Deletion: You can permanently delete all local data and cloud-synced data at any time directly inside the app by going to Settings → Reset App & Delete My Data, or by contacting us at moroccodiy@gmail.com.

Log Data

In the case of an error in the app, standard technical information (such as device model, OS version, and crash logs) may be processed by Google Play Services to help improve app stability.

Cookies and Identifiers

The app itself does not use browser cookies. However, third-party SDKs integrated into the app (such as Google AdMob) may use device identifiers (like the Android Advertising ID) to serve relevant ads and prevent fraud.

Third-Party Services

The application uses the following trusted third-party services, each governed by its own Privacy Policy:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob
  • Google Firebase (Authentication & Cloud Firestore)
